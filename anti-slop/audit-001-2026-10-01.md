# Website design audit 001 (2026-10-01)

Scope: holysmokesbbqco.com homepage (`website/public-landing/index.html`) plus the
Board city SEO pages. Method: web-design skill, antislop Mode 2 (audit, then
Joel picks numbers; nothing changes until he does). Evidence is measured from
the live page via DOM inspection at 1024px and 375px, the skill's
`sitecheck.py` scanner, and the code behind each numeric claim.

**Design Read:** a brand landing page for home pitmasters and BBQ-restaurant
hunters, mostly arriving from store listings on phones, in a smokehouse-sign
visual language (near-black, cream, burnt orange, condensed caps), dial
ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. Impeccable mode: Persuade.

**Direction:** no DESIGN.md exists (R-37). Values below are extracted from the
live CSS and are "inherited", not confirmed brand. Marketing has been asked for
voice, fonts, palette and off-limits items; item 0 depends on that reply.

Priority: HIGH = Hard Gate rule, MED = Purpose-Gate or Impeccable ban,
LOW = Quality Lock / craft floor.

## 1. "100+ cities across 24 states" is wrong by one (R-17) — HIGH
`website/src/board/schema.js` REGIONS covers 23 states (AL AR AZ CO FL GA ID IL
IN KS KY LA MN MO NC NE OH OK PA SC TN TX WI) and 109 cities. Appears in the
Board card and the Board SEO paragraph. Fix: "23 states". (Other numbers
verified true: 11 thermometer apps = 11 parser labels; 16 wood species = 16
WOODS entries; 10 categories = 7 BBQ + 3 family.)

## 2. Tap targets under 44px on mobile (R-03) — HIGH
Measured at 375px: store mini-buttons 33–34px tall (×4), Facebook/Instagram
links 22px, footer links (Privacy, Changelog, Delete account) 14px. Fix:
min-height 44px on `.play-mini`, the social links, and footer links (padding,
not font size).

## 3. Launch buttons fail AA contrast (R-25) — HIGH
White (#fff) on orange #d4782f = 3.22:1 at 14px bold; AA needs 4.5:1 (14px
bold is not "large"). Same risk on the brick and brown app buttons. Fix: dark
text #1a1a1a on the orange (6.5:1), or darken the button fills. Every other
measured pair passes (lowest: footer 4.91:1, tagline 6.11:1).

## 4. No landmarks or skip link (R-32, craft floor) — MED
`<main>` and `<footer>` exist; no `<header>`, no `<nav>`, no skip-to-content
link. Keyboard users land in the hero with no way past it. Fix: wrap hero in
`<header>`, add a visually-hidden skip link, give the app grid a `<nav
aria-label="Apps">`.

## 5. Eyebrow labels above headings (Impeccable ban, scanner MED) — MED
"JUST LAUNCHED" over "NOW ON GOOGLE PLAY", "NEW" over "BBQ BOARD", "INTERNAL
TOOL" over "BBQ CATERING". All three are stale too: Board launched in July and
all three apps are on Play now. Fix: delete the kickers; fold "Internal tool,
sign-in required" into the Catering card's first sentence.

## 6. The launch-announcement block is out of date and duplicates the cards (C-3, R-05) — MED
"Now on Google Play" with three Play badges sits between the story and the
grid, but every card below already carries its own store buttons, including
the App Store. Fix: remove the block; or replace with one short "Get the
apps" row (Play ×3, App Store ×1) and strip store buttons from the cards.
Pick one home for store buttons, not two.

## 7. Five identical cards (R-14) — MED
Same icon-top, h2, paragraph, button on all five; heights 417/417/439/439/333.
Content has a hierarchy the layout hides: Scorecard (both stores) and Board
(newest) lead; Calculator is the no-account instant tool; Catering is internal
and asks visitors to sign in. Fix: lead row with Scorecard and Board at full
width or 2-up, Notebook and Calculator 2-up, Catering demoted to a one-line
footer link ("Catering clients: sign in").

## 8. Hero lacks presence; body measure far too wide (typeset) — MED
h1 is 38px at 1024px with a 200px logo above it; "Faith, Family & Fire" is
gray, tracked caps. The story paragraph runs 940px at 14px ≈ 134 characters
per line (target 65–75). Fix: h1 `clamp(40px, 6vw, 64px)`, logo 160px desktop
/ 120px mobile, tagline sentence case in cream, story `max-width: 68ch` at
16–17px, `text-wrap: balance` on headings.

## 9. Base type is small (typeset) — MED
Body 14px, card copy 13px, footer 11px, kicker 11px. Marketing page, not a
dashboard. Fix: 16px base, 15px card copy, 13px footer minimum.

## 10. Uppercase + wide tracking on everything (R-06) — MED
h1 3px, h2 2px, tagline 2px, kickers 2px, footer 1px, section h2 2px. Oswald
caps is a legitimate smokehouse-sign idiom for headings; it stops being a
choice when the tagline, footer and labels all do it. Fix: keep caps on h1/h2
only, tracking 1px; everything else sentence case, normal tracking.

## 11. Section rhythm (R-05) — MED
Hero → announcement → card grid → social strip → four SEO blocks that
re-describe the same four apps → FAQ → footer; 4,400px tall at desktop,
7,600px on a phone. The SEO blocks exist for search, not readers. Fix: move
"What each app does" below the FAQ (search still indexes it), or shorten each
to two sentences that add facts the cards lack (the 10 categories, the 11
apps, the 16 woods) and delete the overlap.

## 12. Arrow glyph as icon in city-page CTAs (R-08, scanner HIGH) — MED
"Get Live Madison Prices in the App →" on all six Board city pages. Fix: drop
the arrow; the button text names the action already.

## 13. No press state, no reduced-motion fallback (R-19, Emil) — LOW
Buttons have 0.15s hover transitions and nothing on press. Fix: `:active {
transform: translateY(1px) }`, `transition: transform .15s, background .15s`,
and a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` block that zeroes transitions. Hover
only is the right MOTION 1 dial; no scroll animation should be added.

## 14. Browser surfaces unthemed (craft floor) — LOW
No `:focus-visible` rule (default ring only), no `::selection`, default
scrollbar. Fix: 2px cream focus ring with 2px offset on dark; selection
cream-on-orange.

## 15. Palette has six accent-ish colors (R-29) — LOW, needs a written reason
Orange, brass, Notebook green, Board brick, Calculator brown, plus the Play
badge brand colors. Reason that makes it pass: each app owns one color and it
appears only on that app's Launch button and nowhere else on the page. Write
that into DESIGN.md and enforce it (the "NEW" tag currently uses brass on the
Board card, breaking the rule; item 5 removes it).

## 16. Oversized logo sources (optimize) — LOW
Board logo served at 1080px for an 84px slot; Calculator 512px; Holy Smokes
520px for 84px. Fix: 2x-sized PNG/AVIF per slot (168px), as the hero logo
already does.

## 17. Mobile first screen is the logo and headline only — LOW
At 375×812 the story starts at y=440 under a 200px logo and a two-line h1;
fine, but with item 8's smaller logo the bio's first line lands above the
fold, which is the thing worth reading first.
